SEARCH-ENGINE — OPEN YOUR COURSE

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the search-engine folder.
3. Double-click index.html.

That is it. No terminal, Node.js, localhost, or server.
You can also keep index.html as a single file on your Desktop.
If it opens in an editor, right-click > Open with > your web browser.

All seven core lessons and experiments are included offline.
The optional pretrained model in Day 2 and external reading links need internet.

Keep a backup of your notes/progress using My builder notes > Back up progress.
Save it before moving or renaming the HTML file, or clearing browser data.

Developers: see README.md. The modular source, tests, examples, and Pages
workflow remain included; the app has not been published to your account.
